How to play

Press Start on the title screen. Pick a battle style (singles, doubles or a tag battle), your trainer, then your opponent: one of the others, a random one, or the Elite Four, five trainers in a row. Every team is level 100.

Keyboard

←↑↓→
Move the cursor, walk
X
A button: confirm, talk
Z
B button: back, cancel
Enter
Start
Right Shift
Select: credits, on the battle style screen

On a phone or tablet, use the buttons under the screen.
